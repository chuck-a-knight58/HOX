Class Description

Step into the refined world of High Protocol, where service becomes ceremony and presence becomes power. Orchestrated Elegance is more than just a class—it's an immersive experience designed to help you navigate High Protocol dinners with poise, purpose, and embodied confidence.

Whether you're a submissive aiming to refine your service, a Dominant seeking to move through formal spaces with command and grace, or someone new to High Protocol who wants to understand how it all flows, this class will guide you through the intricate dance of a formal High Protocol dining event.

What You'll Learn

  • The structure and energy of a High Protocol dinner—from invitation to departure
  • Proper attire, grooming, and personal preparation for formal settings
  • How to enter and exit a space with confidence and reverence
  • Seating etiquette, body language, and refined table manners
  • The roles and expectations of both Dominants and submissives during formal dining
  • How to offer service without interrupting the flow of the event
  • How to read and respond to subtle cues with elegance and grace
  • Tips for staying grounded under pressure and recovering gracefully from mistakes

Whether you're preparing for your first formal dinner or looking to elevate your presence in High Protocol spaces, Orchestrated Elegance will equip you with the knowledge, awareness, and intentionality to move with confidence and leave a lasting impression.

A downloadable companion workbook is available to support your journey with checklists, guided exercises, journaling prompts, and practical tools you can carry into every elegant occasion.

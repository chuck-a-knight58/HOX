Our Mission

About P.E.T.

Power exchange, explored together — healthy, safe, consensual, and constructive.

Who We Are

Power Exchange Together (P.E.T.) is a community initiative designed to support individuals and dynamics who are exploring or actively living power exchange relationships. Through local chapters, we bring people together to exchange ideas, knowledge, and experience in a welcoming, judgement-free environment.

What We Believe

We understand that power exchange relationships differ based on the individuals within their own consensual dynamic. There is no single "right" way to build authority and service — only the way that is healthy, safe, consensual, and constructive for the people involved. Our role is to provide education, community, and support so members can build the dynamic that is right for them.

How Chapters Work

Each P.E.T. chapter is led by local volunteers who host regular meetings, discussions, and educational gatherings. Chapters set their own cadence and topics while sharing a common set of values and access to a growing library of teaching materials and meeting-topic resources maintained by the international leadership.

Get Involved

Ready to connect? Find a chapter near you, or — if you'd like to start or help lead a chapter — apply to join the leadership. Anyone aged 21 or older is welcome.

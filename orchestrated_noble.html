Orchestrated Noble: A Dominants' Discussion on High Protocol

For those who lead with presence, structure, and discernment.
Class Format Options

  • 90 minutes in person only

Class Description

This interactive discussion and hands-on class reinforces the etiquette and protocols you've previously learned, while expanding your skills in appropriate conversation during formal events. You'll explore how to engage in polished, respectful dialogue and how to properly acknowledge and honor the service staff with grace. Through real-time practice and guided conversation, you'll gain confidence in navigating High Protocol settings with elegance, poise, and mutual respect.

What You'll Learn:

  • The Foundations of High Protocol Events - Understanding the elements that create a seamless experience.
  • The Benefits of a High Protocol Dinner - How it fosters discipline, respect, and a deeper sense of ceremony.
  • Course Structures - Navigating a five or six-course meals
  • Place Setting - Proper table arrangements for a refined dining experience.
  • Meal Etiquette - Expectations and protocols during the meal.
  • The Art of Conversation - Balancing meaningful dialogue, small talk, and appropriate topics of discussion.
  • Wine and Moderation - How much is enough, and how to ensure an elegant experience.
  • Personal Conduct - Leading with poise, confidence, and respect.
  • Event Flow & Final Thoughts - Structuring the evening and refining the High Protocol experience.

This class is ideal for Dominants who wish to enhance their leadership in High Protocol settings, refine their hosting skills, and create an atmosphere of elegance, respect, and appreciation to seamless service.

Orchestrated Service: Royal Service Practicum

For those who serve with elegance, intention, and devotion.
2 hours practicum in person only
Presented by: TheGirl_Star

Class Description

Service is an art, a discipline, and a reflection of dedication and precision. This hands-on training is designed for subs/slaves who wish to refine their skills in High Protocol service. From setting a flawless table for a five- or six-course meal to mastering time management in meal preparation, this class will provide the essential knowledge and practice needed to serve with confidence and grace.

What You'll Learn

  • The Three Pillars of Service - Understanding the foundation of exceptional service.
  • Types of Service - Exploring different service styles and their applications.
  • What Not to Do - Common mistakes and how to avoid them.
  • How to Clear - Proper techniques for clearing dishes seamlessly.
  • How to Serve - The etiquette and execution of serving with precision.
  • Key Reminders - Essential habits and details that elevate service.

This intensive training is designed for those who take pride in service and wish to enhance their skills in a structured and elegant manner. By the end of this class, participants will have a deeper understanding of High Protocol dining service and the ability to execute it with excellence.

About

Xanadu Reserve was created as the natural evolution of our High Protocol work. After nearly every Intensive, we were asked the same question:

“What comes next?”

Xanadu Reserve is our answer.

We are a private, curated social club for individuals and couples who value refinement, discernment, and meaningful connection. Our gatherings from cocktail evenings to immersive dining experiences are intentionally designed to foster presence, cultivated taste, and authentic alignment.

We believe elegance is practiced.
Community is cultivated.
Standards are upheld with grace.

For those seeking depth beyond the event — Xanadu Reserve offers a place to continue your journey.

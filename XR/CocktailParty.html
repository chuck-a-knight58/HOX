Join us for a casually elegant Cocktail Evening designed to welcome new faces while honoring the intimacy and intentionality our community is known for.

This gathering offers a gentle introduction to Xanadu Reserve — a private social club for those who value presence, discernment, elevated experiences, and meaningful connection.

The evening unfolds with quiet elegance. We begin with a warm welcome and shared toast, setting the tone for connection and refinement. From there, the evening opens naturally: guests and members mingle in relaxed, thoughtful conversation, questions are welcomed, and connections form organically.

While this evening welcomes newcomers, it is always held with intention and care. We believe meaningful communities are cultivated slowly — through shared standards, mutual respect, and genuine presence.

We look forward to meeting you.

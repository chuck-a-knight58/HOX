The Experience A Starry Night in Paris
Your evening begins with focused instruction on the foundations of High Protocol dining, embodied etiquette, and the subtle art of moving with intention. Beneath a Parisian-inspired ambiance, you will engage in guided practice, ritual structure, and hands-on refinement designed to cultivate composure, grace, and confident presence. Every gesture is considered. Every movement purposeful. This is where protocol becomes poetry in motion.

As twilight deepens into a velvet sky, the experience transitions into an exquisite multi-course dinner curated by a professional chef. Inspired by the romance of Paris and the artistry of French cuisine, the menu unfolds beneath a canopy of soft candlelight and starlit elegance. Deep midnight hues, shimmering accents, and refined décor evoke the charm of a summer evening along the Seine—creating a dining experience that feels both intimate and transcendent.

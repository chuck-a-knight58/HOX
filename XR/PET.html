Power Exchange Together North Central NJ is an organization committed to supporting participants who have a desire to explore power-exchange relationships in a healthy, safe, consensual, and constructive way — by exchanging ideas, knowledge, and experience.

We understand that power-exchange relationships differ based on the individuals within their own consensual dynamic. So, join us each month as we welcome everyone into the world of power exchange.

For more information on monthly in-person meetings, visit our FetLife group.

Anyone over 21 is welcome.

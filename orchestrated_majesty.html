Offered exclusively in person, Orchestrated Majesty invites participants to experience a full-day intensive designed to deepen their understanding of High Protocol through education, embodiment, and elegance.

This immersive offering features three signature modules—Orchestrated Elegance, Orchestrated Grace, and Orchestrated Noble—culminating in a formal, multi-course High Protocol dinner that puts your learning into refined practice.

What This Intensive Offers:

  • A one-day, in-person experience tailored to offer your participants direct engagement in High Protocol rituals, etiquette, and presence.

  • A formal multi-course dining experience where every detail is intentionally orchestrated to model and embody High Protocol from start to finish.

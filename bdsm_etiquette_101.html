Class Description

Whether you're stepping into your first munch or navigating a high-energy dungeon party, understanding community etiquette is essential to creating safe, respectful, and empowering spaces. This engaging, interactive class introduces the core principles of BDSM etiquette—covering both public and private play settings—with a focus on grace, mutual respect, and situational awareness.

Participants will learn how to navigate common social and scene-related challenges with confidence and clarity. We'll explore best practices for dungeon and social events, how to engage online without overstepping, and how to uphold personal boundaries while honoring others'. Using real-world scenarios, role-plays, and open discussion, this class goes beyond rules—it helps cultivate the mindset of an aware and considerate community member.

Topics Covered:

  • Core etiquette principles: Consent, communication, and conduct
  • Social events: Munches, mixers, and inclusivity
  • Dungeon etiquette: Scenes, play space protocols, and DM authority
  • Online behavior: Messaging, boundaries, and digital respect
  • Conflict management and bystander intervention
  • Situational responses: Role-play and debrief (e.g., uninvited kneeling, scene interruptions, consent breaches)

By the end of this class, participants will walk away with a deeper understanding of how to move through the kink community with poise, respect, and authenticity—and how their actions can uplift and protect the space for everyone.

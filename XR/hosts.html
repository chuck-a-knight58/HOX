Meet the Hosts

“Xanadu was not built as a business. It was built as a home for those who recognize each other.”

Xanadu Reserve is the work of two people who have spent years refining what ritual, service, and elegance mean in the kink community. Every gathering reflects their shared practice and their conviction that how we come together matters as much as what we do.

Knight of Xanadu
Co-Founder · Host

Knight of Xanadu is a long-time practitioner and educator in power-exchange dynamics, High Protocol, and the art of intentional community. He brings a steady, structured presence to Reserve events and the kind of presence that makes room for everyone else to be fully themselves.

His work across Xanadu Kink, Xanadu Kink Academy, and Power Exchange Together reflects a belief that education and ritual are inseparable: structure is what makes depth possible.

The Girl_Star
Co-Founder · Host

The Girl_Star is the heart of Xanadu’s warmth. Her attention to the smallest details (a folded napkin, a warmed glass, a moment of eye contact at the door) turns a gathering into an experience.

Her practice of service, presence, and deliberate care shapes the texture of every Reserve event. What she embodies, others are invited into.

Why Xanadu?

Many years ago, we came across the word Xanadu and were captivated by its meaning: a place of great luxury, contentment, and beauty. The more we lived with it, the more it spoke to us; not as a reference, but as a vision.

Luxury is not wealth. It is being genuinely cared for. The quiet warmth of someone noticing whether you are warm enough. The opposite of the false exclusivity that withholds kindness based on appearance.

Contentment is having what you need. Peace. The knowledge that you are safe, that you are enough, and that you are surrounded by people who accept you fully.

Beauty comes from within. It is not about how you look. It is about who you are. We are all beautiful in our own unique way.

That is what we are building, one gathering at a time.

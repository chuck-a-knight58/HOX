Enchanted Waters is a poolside gathering for Xanadu Reserve and Club InnThrall members. Join us for an afternoon where fantasy meets refinement, and every ripple invites connection, laughter, and a touch of enchantment.

Set against the backdrop of an Olympic-sized pool, guests are invited to swim, relax, and play in an atmosphere designed for both leisure and connection. Enjoy curated mocktails and light bites as you bask in the sun, embody your most radiant self, and perhaps even channel your inner mermaid.

Whether you float, mingle, or simply soak in the ambiance, this experience is designed to be both refreshing and unforgettable.

Also included: hors d’oeuvres and mocktails. BYO legal libations welcome.

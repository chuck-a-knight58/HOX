Requirements to Become a Vetted Guest
To be considered a vetted guest of Escape to Xanadu, the following requirements must be met in full:


Good Standing Within the Community
The prospective guest must be known within the broader kink or power exchange community and have no unresolved concerns, patterns of misconduct, or negative reputation that would conflict with the values of the house.


Sponsorship Requirement
Each prospective guest must have an approved sponsor who is already vetted and in good standing with Escape to Xanadu. The sponsor is responsible for mentorship, guidance, and accountability throughout the vetting period.


Attendance at a Minimum of Two Parties With Sponsor
The prospective guest must attend at least two Escape to Xanadu events accompanied by their sponsor. These events are observational and participatory within agreed-upon limits.


Completion of Two Play Parties With Open-Door Scenes
The prospective guest must participate in two play parties where all scenes are conducted with open doors. This allows for transparency, safety oversight, and evaluation of etiquette, consent practices, and scene conduct.


No Violations During the Sponsorship Period
No violations of house rules, consent boundaries, protocol expectations, or community standards may occur during the sponsorship timeframe. Any violation may result in immediate termination of the vetting process.

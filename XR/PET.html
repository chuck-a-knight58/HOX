Power Exchange Together North Central NJ is an organization that is committed to supporting participants that have a desire to explore power exchange relationships in a healthy, safe, consensual, and constructive way by exchanging ideas, knowledge and experience.

We understand that power exchange relationships differ based on the individuals within their own consensual dynamic.

So, join us each month as we welcome everyone into the world of power exchange

For more information on monthly in person meetings visit our FETLife page.

Class Description

Do you long to be cherished, adored, and treated like royalty while embracing your kinky side? Whether you're a pampered brat, an elegant consort, or a graceful submissive, this class will teach you how to embody the essence of a Kinky Princess. We'll explore confidence, etiquette, self-care, and power dynamics that enhance your royal role—whether you're ruling a personal kingdom or surrendering with poise.

Come ready to refine your presence, master the art of receiving devotion, and wield your princess power with grace and confidence.

Xanadu Reserve Membership

Xanadu Reserve is a private social club for those who value refined luxury, intentional connection, and the embodied practice of High Protocol.

Xanadu Reserve is not simply about access it is about stepping into a shared practice of ritual, presence, and elevated community.

Within Xanadu Reserve, structure is honored, connection is cultivated, and each gathering reflects our commitment to grace, refinement, and belonging.

Annual Membership Fee:

$100 per couple annually (charter member lifetime rate)
$50 for singles annually (charter member lifetime rate)

​Application Process:

Complete the application here. Our Advisory Board reviews each submission and may request an interview or invite you to a meet-and-greet. Approved applicants will receive a membership link, followed by a welcome email once their membership is secured.

Membership Benefits

✨ Priority Access to Events
Members receive early registration and first right of refusal on all Xanadu Reserve gatherings, including High Protocol dinners, Royal Afternoon Teas, Reserve Cocktail parties, and curated social experiences. Most Reserve events are members-only. When select events welcome non-members, Reserve members always receive first priority.

✨ Members may bring up to 2 guests to any members only event
This is a great way for you to introduce your friends to Xanadu Reserve

✨ Exclusive Xanadu Reserve Pin (Required for Entry)
Each member receives an official Xanadu Reserve Pin, worn at all Reserve events as a symbol of belonging and verification of membership.

✨ Digital Resource Access
Members receive access to curated High Protocol materials, including etiquette refreshers, ritual guidance, and event preparation resources.

✨ Member Recognition
Membership includes acknowledgment within our community through anniversary recognition, member spotlights.

## ✨ Why the name Xanadu? ✨
*Shared from the heart 💜*

Many years ago, I came across the word Xanadu and was instantly captivated by its meaning:
*A place of great luxury, contentment, and beauty.*

The more I explored it, the more it spoke to me—not as a movie reference, but as a vision for the kind of space I wanted to create in life and community.

Here’s what each part means to me:

💎 **Luxury**  is not defined by wealth or possessions; it is a feeling. It is the quiet warmth of being genuinely cared for, someone taking a moment to ensure your comfort, like a vendor offering cocoa on a cold day and asking, “Are you warm enough?” That is true luxury: to be seen, acknowledged, and valued. It stands in sharp contrast to the kind of false exclusivity that withholds kindness based on appearance. an attitude memorably captured in Pretty Woman.

🌿 **Contentment** is having everything you need, not everything you want. It’s peace. It’s knowing you’re safe, that you’re enough, and that you’re surrounded by people who accept you fully.

🌟 **Beauty** comes from within. It’s not about how you look, but about who you are. We’re all beautiful in our own unique ways, and that beauty shines brightest when we are allowed to be fully ourselves.

So, when I chose the name **Xanadu**, I was dreaming of a safe space, not physical, but emotional and spiritual. Where people could feel luxury (being valued), contentment (inner peace), and beauty (self-worth).

That dream has become the foundation of everything we’re building.

Thank you for being part of this journey and reading all the way to the end.

Let’s keep creating that magic—together. 🗝💜

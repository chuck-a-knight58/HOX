P.E.T. is a community initiative designed to support individuals and dynamics who are exploring or actively living power exchange relationships. Our chapters gather to exchange ideas, knowledge, and experience — helping members build power exchange dynamics that are healthy, safe, consensual, and constructive.

We understand that every power exchange relationship differs based on the individuals within their own consensual dynamic. This site is the home of P.E.T.'s international chapter leadership: a place to find your local chapter, and — for chapter leaders — a shared library of teaching materials, meeting-topic notes, and presentation resources.

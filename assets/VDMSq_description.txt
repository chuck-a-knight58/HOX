Power Exchange Together — Dominant Group


Join @DimpledSadist and @Knight_of_Xanadu for our next virtual gathering!


We’re creating a welcoming, respectful space for adults on the left side of the slash to share experiences, ask questions, and learn from one another. Switches are welcome when participating from their Dominant side.


For our first meeting, we’ll get acquainted, explore what dominance means to each of us, and hear what you’d like from future gatherings. Whether you’re new to leadership or have years of experience, your perspective is welcome.


Monthly topics will help start the conversation, but this is your group. Your questions and experiences will guide the discussion. Listening is participation, too; there’s no pressure to share before you’re comfortable.


Guidelines for Our Time Together


•	Respect and confidentiality: Personal stories, names, identities, and identifying details shared during our meetings must remain within the group. No recording, screenshots, or photographs.


•	Cameras on: Please keep your camera on during the meeting so we can see who is participating and build connection. If you need to step away briefly, let a host know.


•	A private space: Join from a private location where others cannot see or overhear the discussion. Only approved attendees may be present, and no minors may be within sight or hearing of the meeting. Please use headphones when needed.


•	Room for every voice: Speak from your own experience, allow others to finish, and share the conversation. Ask before offering advice, and respect someone’s choice to listen or pass on a question.


•	Respect different dynamics: Dominance takes many forms. We can learn from one another without judging or insisting that someone else’s relationship should look like our own.


•	Connection without pressure: This is a discussion and support space, not a place to seek partners, solicit play, or send unwanted advances. Participation does not imply consent to private messages.


•	Care for yourself: Share only what feels comfortable. You may take a break or step away whenever you need to, and you’re welcome to contact a host privately with concerns.


Please Honor Your RSVP


Please update your RSVP or message us if you can no longer attend so we can offer your place to someone else. Repeated no-shows without communication may affect approval for future meetings.


Meeting Access


The meeting link will be sent privately to attendees who have RSVP’d as going on the day of the gathering. Please do not share or forward it.


We’ll meet online monthly on the 4th Thursday of the month.
